Returns the KRX price grid for a kind of security listed on KRX.

Stocks, foreign stocks, depositary receipts, infrastructure funds and REITs get the stock table, and ETFs and ETNs the ETF and ETN table. Foreign ETFs, warrants and unknown types get an UNKNOWN rule.
