Detailed Description

The summary of a replay; read halted_ and caveats_ before the figures.

Unobserved figures follow the analytics conventions (missing(), math::undefined()).

◆ halted_

std::optional<BrokerError> algotradelib::backtest::Report::halted_

Why the replay stopped early, if it did; read this before any figure.

Set when an execution could not be costed, the ledger could not record one, or positions could not be marked at a bar's close (mark_not_representable). The figures cover the bars up to that point; the halting bar is counted in bars_, and fills settled on it before the refusal are included. Nothing ran after the refusal, and on_stop was not called.
