Detailed Description

A blocking HTTP/1.1 client over TLS that keeps one connection to one host.

Not paced, not thread-safe, and with no enforced deadlines: a blocking DNS lookup, connect, TLS handshake, write, read, or the TLS shutdown when closing can wait indefinitely. Broker clients use AsyncHttpClient.
