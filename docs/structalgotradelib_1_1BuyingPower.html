Detailed Description

What the broker says may be spent, in one currency.

The broker's own figure (Toss cashBuyingPower, KIS nrcvb_buy_amt): it counts unsettled proceeds on the broker's terms, excludes amounts reserved for resting orders, and includes no credit. It is not the library's own Portfolio::cash_. In a backtest the ledger fills it with modelled cash.
