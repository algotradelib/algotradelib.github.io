Detailed Description

How a backtest charges commission: one stated model, or a schedule resolved per execution.

stated charges one CommissionModel throughout. scheduled picks the rate for each execution by its market, its local trading date and the venue the caller asserts (a replay cannot know where an order executed, so the report lists the venue as an assumption). A rate that cannot be resolved is an error, and the replay halts rather than skipping or zero-costing the fill.
