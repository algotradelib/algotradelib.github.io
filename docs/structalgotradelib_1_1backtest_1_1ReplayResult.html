◆ halted_

std::optional<BrokerError> algotradelib::backtest::ReplayResult::halted_

Why the replay stopped early, if it did.

Set when an execution could not be costed (no rate for the date or venue, or a market whose trading day the replay cannot compute), the ledger could not record one, or a position could not be marked at a bar's close (mark_not_representable). Every figure then covers the bars up to the halt; the halting bar is counted in bars_, but nothing after the refusal ran on it.

◆ overdrawn_bars_

std::size_t algotradelib::backtest::ReplayResult::overdrawn_bars_ {0}

Bars that closed with cash below zero in currency_ (counted per bar fed, so two instruments count both).

Under the default limits a replay borrows without limit: nothing refuses an unaffordable buy unless RiskLimits::enforce_buying_power_ is on, and even then a market buy is checked at the reference price without commission and can overdraw on a gap. Short proceeds count as cash. With negative starting cash every bar counts, while lowest_cash_ (set by executions) may stay empty.

◆ unchecked_fill_causality_

std::size_t algotradelib::backtest::ReplayResult::unchecked_fill_causality_ {0}

How many fill decisions could not be checked for causality; see ReportCaveats::unchecked_fill_causality_.

Besides quotes, an order decided on one interval's bar and filled against another interval's bar of the same instrument (a Tuesday daily bar against that week's weekly bar) can be one. Separately, the nominal period end of an open-stamped monthly or yearly bar also dates its fills and equity points, so a February fill can be dated in March.

◆ unvalued_bars_

std::size_t algotradelib::backtest::ReplayResult::unvalued_bars_ {0}

Bars whose equity total did not fit an exact decimal, so they have no curve point.

The next point carries no return. The replay and its callbacks continue, with per-lot and per-currency figures available, but the missing total also means no portfolio value for weight sizing, so a weight-sized intent on such a bar is rejected (NO_PORTFOLIO_VALUE).
