Detailed Description

A market in memory: bars the caller gives, a clock the caller moves, one account's cash, holdings and orders.

A bar is applied when the clock passes its period end. Orders fill by the backtest's rule (backtest::NextBarFillModel): an order trades against the first bar of its instrument whose period began at or after the instant the market accepted it, a market order at that bar's open, a limit order if the bar's range reaches the limit, never partly; the fill is stamped at the bar's period end. A DAY order lives in the session of that first bar, and expires as a backtest's does: after a daily bar of its session, after an intraday bar ending at or after the session's close, or before a bar of a later date (a session whose closing bar is missing still ended). An immediate-or-cancel or fill-or-kill order expires after its first bar. Expired orders are CANCELED.

Unlike a backtest, the market never fills an order against a bar that began before the order was accepted, even where a backtest cannot tell and allows it (ReplayResult::unchecked_fill_causality_).

Not modelled: trading hours (an order is accepted at any instant; the calendar and the session window only date the market), price bands, partial fills, volume, settlement, short selling, interest. Cash is kept in one currency, and may go below zero when a market buy fills above the price it reserved.

If a fill cannot be costed (the fill model's commission has no rate for its date) or recorded (its amount does not fit an exact decimal), or a position cannot be valued at a bar's close, the market halts: advance_to throws std::runtime_error and keeps throwing, placements are refused (market_halted), and the clock stays at the period end of the bar it halted on. What was done before the failure stands, including fills of earlier orders on that bar; the order that failed stays PENDING. Reads answer that state.

Not thread-safe; nothing is persisted.
