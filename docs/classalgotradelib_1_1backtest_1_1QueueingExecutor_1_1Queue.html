template<typename BrokerTag>
class algotradelib::backtest::QueueingExecutor< BrokerTag >::Queue

Every outstanding order, oldest first: a view over the queue, valid until the queue changes.

Iterated, counted (size, empty) and read from the front (front); it holds no copy of any order.
