TradingCalendar algotradelib::backtest::ReplayConfig::calendar_

The calendar ctx.calendar() returns, for a strategy asking about dates it has no bar for (such as whether tomorrow trades).

Empty by default. It does not change the phase of a fed bar, which comes from feed's trading_day argument.
