Detailed Description

The fill model: market orders at the next bar's open, limit orders when the next bar's range reaches them, stop-limit orders once released.

Works the same for any bar interval. Volume is not consulted: a fill is never partial or capped by what traded (the report states this).

Decides whether an order trades against a bar, and at what price and cost.

  • Market: the bar's open (its close if it has no open).
  • Limit: only if the bar reached the limit (low at or below a buy limit, high at or above a sell limit). Credited the limit, or under AT_OPEN_IF_BETTER the open if better; the credit is kept within the bar's range and never through the limit, even after slippage.
  • Stop-limit: dormant until the bar reaches the trigger (a sell stop when the low reaches it, a buy stop when the high does). Released at the open, it is priced as a limit. Released inside the bar, it fills only when the bar proves a price after the release: at the worst bar price the limit allows if the limit was marketable at the trigger, at the limit if the close is through the limit; otherwise it rests. No favourable slippage applies after an intrabar release.

Slippage, then commission and any sale tax, are applied to a fill. The execution is stamped at next.period_end_.

Parameters
orderThe queued order.
nextThe bar after the one the order was decided on.
ruleThe instrument's price grid, for tick slippage.
dateThe market-local trading date of the fill, for the commission rate (see SessionWindow::local_date).
open_dateThe market-local date of the bar's open, if known; used instead of date for a fill priced at the open. For a fill inside a multi-day bar date is used, which can be a later day than the fill.
releasedWhether an earlier bar already released this stop-limit order.
Returns
The decision. A stop-limit order not yet released without both a trigger and a limit price neither trades nor rests (a released stop must still have its limit price).

NextBarFillModel algotradelib::backtest::NextBarFillModel::with_sell_tax ( SellTax tax) const

Returns a copy of this model that charges a tax on sales.

The library has no tax rate of its own (Korean securities transaction tax changes yearly); one rate applies to the whole replay. The tax is truncated to whole currency units and reported apart from commission (Execution::tax_, Report::tax_paid_). Only sales of instruments in tax.country_ pay it; exempt instruments (Korean ETFs and ETNs) need a replay without it.
