Detailed Description

A price known to be on its instrument's tick grid.

Make one with checked (for a price you have) or rounded (to move a price onto the grid). For a price computed as a Scalar, use analytics::round_scalar_to_tick first.

Makes a price by rounding a value onto the grid, in the order's favour.

A buy rounds down and a sell rounds up, so the price never becomes worse for the order.
