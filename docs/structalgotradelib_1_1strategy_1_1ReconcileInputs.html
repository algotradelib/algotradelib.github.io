Detailed Description

What reconciliation needs besides the intent and the portfolio; the runner fills it in.

A missing input refuses the instructions that need it, with a RejectReason that names it.

◆ reserved_rows_are_unconfirmed_

bool algotradelib::strategy::ReconcileInputs::reserved_rows_are_unconfirmed_ {false}

Whether the portfolio's reserved rows (WorkingOrder::reserved_) are orders this runner sent that the holdings may not reflect yet, and which may already have filled.

When set, a target that finds too much working cancels that side and sizes its replacement net of those rows, so a filled reservation is not traded twice.
