Detailed Description

Commission and tax the broker stated on the orders a loop happened to poll (Toss states them; KIS does not).

A subtotal of unknown coverage, not a total: an order that fills and leaves the working-order listing between two polls, or is placed and filled between them, is never seen with its final cost.
