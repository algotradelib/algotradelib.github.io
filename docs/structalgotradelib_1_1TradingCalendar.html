Detailed Description

The trading days a broker reported for one market, ascending by date.

A calendar covers a finite set of dates. is_trading_day tells "closed" apart from "not covered": a date the broker did not report, or one whose row could not be read, has no answer rather than a guessed one. The searches return reported days only, and do not say whether unreported dates are open.

Keep days_ ascending if you fill it yourself.

Intraday session hours are not part of this type, and the library does not expose them yet (Toss publishes them).

◆ unreadable_days_

std::size_t algotradelib::TradingCalendar::unreadable_days_ {0}

How many rows from the broker had a date that could not be read.

Each such row is dropped, and the day it described is uncovered: is_trading_day returns nothing for it.

Warning
The dropped day can fall inside the covered span. When this count is nonzero, next_trading_day and previous_trading_day may skip over a trading day.
