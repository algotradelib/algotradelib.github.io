strong

Who or what is holding trading stopped.

Several holds can be in force at once; trading resumes only when every one is released. The enumerators are ordered most fundamental first, and RiskGuard::blocking_reason reports the first one held. Whether a hold also stops cancellations is halt_blocks.

Enumerator
OPERATOR 

A person stopped trading with RiskGuard::halt(); only RiskGuard::resume() clears it.

LOCAL_FAILURE 

Local machinery failed, such as the order journal not being writable; nothing is sent.

Sticky; release it with runner::Runner::resume_after_local_failure, which also acknowledges the journal failures (RiskGuard::resume(LOCAL_FAILURE) lifts the hold without that). The detail names the failure.

STOPPING 

A stop was requested and the run is shutting down.

ORDER_LIST_UNREAD 

The broker's order list has not been read since this process started.

Until it is, the runner cannot know whether a previous process left orders working, so it places nothing.

PORTFOLIO_ABSENT 

No holdings snapshot has been read yet, so what the account holds is unknown.

An empty portfolio would pass every check as if the account were flat, so placements wait.

PORTFOLIO_STALE

BUYING_POWER_UNREAD

POLL_FAILING

◆ RejectReason

enum class algotradelib::strategy::RejectReason
strong

Why an instruction was not placed.

Most reasons mean reconciliation could not build a valid instruction. BROKER_REFUSED and BINDING_REFUSED mean it was built and then refused.

Enumerator
NO_REFERENCE_DATA 

No reference data for the instrument, so neither the tick grid nor the lot is known.

UNKNOWN_PRICE_GRID 

The instrument's tick grid is unknown, so a limit price cannot be made valid.

NO_PRICE_AVAILABLE 

A price was needed to size the order and none was available.

NO_PORTFOLIO_VALUE 

A weight needed the portfolio's value, and none (or a non-positive one, or one in another currency) was supplied.

QUANTITY_ROUNDS_TO_ZERO 

The quantity rounded down to zero on the lot.

NOT_REPRESENTABLE 

The decimal arithmetic did not fit, or the portfolio's rows did not add up.

NO_PERMITTED_PRICE 

Rounding the limit onto the grid, in the cautious direction, would move it out of the day's band: no tick lies between the band's bound and the limit (clamped into the band).

It takes a band with no tick on that side of the limit; on KRX, for example, only a band narrower than one tick, at a price of a few won. A limit the strategy itself set beyond the band (a buy below the lower bound, a sell above the upper) is left alone, as without a band.

BROKER_REFUSED 

The broker refused the instruction (a request was sent).

BINDING_REFUSED 

The binding refused to send the instruction, so no request was made.

For example, an idempotency key at a broker that accepts none, or a cancellation of an order the broker has no reference for.

TRIGGER_OFF_GRID 

A stop-limit order's trigger price is not on the instrument's tick grid.

Unlike a limit price, a trigger is never rounded, since rounding would change when the order is released.

STOP_ORDER_RESTING 

A target needs a trade while a stop order rests on the instrument; the stop is cancelled first and nothing is placed this pass.

runner::Runner keeps the target and settles it again once no stop is listed on the instrument (see Runner::deferred_targets); a newer instruction for the instrument replaces it. A target the holding already meets is not refused, and the stop stays.

UNSUPPORTED_WEIGHT 

A weight-sized intent with something a holding cannot express: a sell, a negative weight, a trigger, a validity other than DAY, or an idempotency key.

NEGATIVE_SIZE 

An intent's size is negative: a quantity or a notional amount below zero.

An intent carries its direction in its side, so a negative size is a sign error, and an order of -5 shares is not a sell of 5. With the inputs it needs available, a zero quantity or notional is QUANTITY_ROUNDS_TO_ZERO; a negative weight is UNSUPPORTED_WEIGHT.

◆ projected_gross_exposure()

template<typename Broker>
std::optional< Decimal64 > algotradelib::strategy::projected_gross_exposure ( const Portfolio< Broker > & portfolio,
const std::vector< InstrumentBasis > & basis,

const PlannedOrder * candidate,
const std::optional< Decimal64 > & candidate_price,

Computes the gross exposure in one currency if every outstanding order (and a candidate) filled.

Per instrument, the exposure is max(|held + working buys|, |held - working sells|) times a price, summed over instruments in currency. Opposite-side orders do not offset each other. The price is the highest that could apply: the basis mark and the limit price of every open order on the instrument, the candidate's included. The candidate joins its own instrument on its own side, so a sell that exits a long up to flat (counting sells already working) does not raise the figure, though it does not lower it either until it fills. A sell larger than the long reaches a short and can raise it.

Template Parameters
BrokerThe portfolio's broker tag.
Parameters
portfolioThe positions (summed over every row) and working orders.
basisThe currency and mark of every instrument held, working or ordered.
currencyThe currency to total; instruments in other currencies contribute nothing.
candidateThe order being checked, or nullptr.
candidate_priceThe price to value the candidate at if it has no limit (a market order).
contributionWhether the candidate's quantity counts, or only its price.
Returns
The exposure, or std::nullopt if it cannot be computed exactly: an instrument missing from basis or listed twice, a mark or price that is not positive, a working order filled beyond its quantity, or arithmetic that does not fit.

◆ reconcile() [2/2]

template<typename Broker>
Plan< Broker > algotradelib::strategy::reconcile ( const TargetPosition & target,
const Portfolio< Broker > & portfolio,
const ReconcileInputs & inputs )

Plans the cancellations and the order that move a holding to a target position.

  1. The need is the target minus the net quantity held (every row).
  2. Open orders working against the need are cancelled; if the target is already met, every open order on the instrument is.
  3. What is already working on the needed side is subtracted from the need.
  4. If more is working than needed, that whole side is cancelled and the remainder placed fresh.
  5. The order is rounded down onto the lot, and its limit (if the target has one) clamped where it is more aggressive than the band allows, and rounded; without a limit it is a market order.

When a stop order rests on the instrument, the steps above do not apply:

  • if a trade is needed, the plan cancels the stop (unless its cancellation is already pending) and also carries a RejectReason::STOP_ORDER_RESTING rejection, with nothing placed;
  • if the target is already met, the stop is kept and only the other open orders are cancelled.

A drift below ReconcileInputs::tolerance_ yields an empty plan, but only when nothing has to be cancelled; an order working against the target is still cancelled.

Template Parameters
BrokerThe broker tag.
Parameters
targetThe target position.
portfolioThe positions and working orders.
inputsReference data, prices, band and tolerance.
Returns
The plan. A refusal for any reason other than STOP_ORDER_RESTING clears the cancellations and placements and carries one Rejection; with STOP_ORDER_RESTING the cancellations stay, and the caller should send them.

◆ weight_target()

WeightTarget algotradelib::strategy::weight_target ( const OrderIntent & intent,
const ReconcileInputs & inputs )
inline

Converts a weight-sized intent into the target position it states.

The target is weight * portfolio value / price shares, rounded down onto the lot, where the price is the intent's limit if it has one and the reference price otherwise. The target keeps the intent's limit and tag. Reconciled as a target, its limit applies to whatever trade the difference needs, so above the weight a buy limit becomes the limit of a sell.

Parameters
intentA weight-sized intent (SizeKind::TARGET_WEIGHT).
inputsReference data, reference price, and portfolio value.
Returns
The target, or a refusal: UNSUPPORTED_WEIGHT (sell, negative weight, trigger, non-DAY, idempotency key), NO_REFERENCE_DATA, NO_PORTFOLIO_VALUE (absent, not positive, or in another currency), NO_PRICE_AVAILABLE, or NOT_REPRESENTABLE.
Exceptions
std::logic_errorif intent is not weight-sized.
