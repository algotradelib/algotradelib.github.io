◆ order_amount_

std::optional<Decimal64> algotradelib::OrderSnapshot::order_amount_

The money an amount-based order committed (Toss US market orders), if any.

The library does not place such orders, but reports ones placed elsewhere. Toss can state the amount with more digits than Decimal64 holds; it is then rounded half to even to 18 significant digits.

◆ trigger_price_

std::optional<Decimal64> algotradelib::OrderSnapshot::trigger_price_

The trigger price, whenever the broker reports a positive one.

Kept even on a row whose type is not STOP_LIMIT (a broker contradiction); replacing such an order is then refused, while cancelling it works. Empty on a stop row means the broker did not say, and replacing it is refused.
