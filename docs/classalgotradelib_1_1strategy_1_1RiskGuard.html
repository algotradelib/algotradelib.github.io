Detailed Description

Checks planned orders against RiskLimits, holds trading stopped, and counts orders sent.

The rate limit counts what was recorded with note_sent. The runner records every placement or cancellation that was sent or may have been sent: accepted, rejected by the broker, or of unknown outcome. A request refused before sending (by this guard, the binding or the adapter) is not recorded, so it uses no budget.

Note
Not thread-safe; one guard belongs to one runner.

◆ halt() [1/3]

void algotradelib::strategy::RiskGuard::halt ( )
inlinenoexcept

Takes the operator's hold (HaltReason::OPERATOR): no order or cancellation is sent until resume().

This only stops sending ("quiesce"): working orders and positions are left as they are. To also cancel working orders or close positions, use runner::StopIntent with runner::PollingLoop, or a strategy such as vanilla::TimedLiquidation. The hold is in memory; runner::FileStopChannel and runner::SignalStop stop a running process from outside.
