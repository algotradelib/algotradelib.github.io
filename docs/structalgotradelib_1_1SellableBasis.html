Detailed Description

What a sellable quantity covers, and how RiskGuard must treat resting sells against it.

Both parts default to UNESTABLISHED, and RiskGuard refuses a checked sell against a figure whose basis is not enforceable. An adapter that fills sellable_quantity_ must state this too.

Whether the figure covers the whole holding.

Enumerator
UNESTABLISHED 

Not established; RiskGuard refuses.

WHOLE_HOLDING 

Every share held of the instrument, whatever venue or lot.

Only part of the holding, such as one venue's shares; RiskGuard refuses, reporting SELLABLE_BASIS_UNESTABLISHED.

How RiskGuard treats resting sell orders against the figure.

Enumerator
UNESTABLISHED 

Not stated; RiskGuard refuses.

DEDUCT 

Subtract every resting sell from the figure.

Safe whatever the broker does: if the broker already deducted them, sells are over-refused, never doubled.
