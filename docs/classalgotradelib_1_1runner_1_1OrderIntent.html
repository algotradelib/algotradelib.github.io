Detailed Description

One order a strategy asks the runner to place.

Make one with limit, market or stop_limit; add a tag with with_tag and an idempotency key with with_client_order_id. Submit it with ctx.submit(intent).

inlinestatic

Makes a stop-limit order: a limit order the broker releases when the trigger price is reached.

Which direction of move reaches the trigger, and how the trigger may relate to the limit, is the broker's rule and is not checked here. A backtest models the release and the fill with stated assumptions and counts them in ReportCaveats; see NextBarFillModel::decide.
