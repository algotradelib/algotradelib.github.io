Detailed Description

template<typename Strategy, typename CapsType = strategy::NoCaps>
class algotradelib::backtest::Backtester< Strategy, CapsType >

Replays bars (and quotes) through one strategy, with a ledger and a fill model.

Feed bars with feed, in timestamp order across instruments, then call finish and summarize the result().

backtest::Backtester<MyStrategy> replay(MyStrategy{}, config);
for (const auto& bar : bars) {
replay.feed(strategy::make_bar_event(id, BarInterval::ONE_DAY, BarStamp::CLOSE, bar));
}
replay.finish();
const auto report = backtest::summarize(replay.result(), replay.fills());

std::optional< bool > trading_day = true )
inline

Replays one bar.

In order: record the previous period's equity point if this bar begins another period (EquityCurve::PER_PERIOD), fill orders resting from earlier bars against this bar (and call on_fill), mark positions at the close (recording this bar's point under EquityCurve::PER_BAR), advance the clock to the bar's period end (calling on_phase on a change), then call on_bar, whose orders wait for a later bar. After a halt it does nothing.

For a strategy declaring on_bars, the bar is cached instead and held with the rest of its period; the period goes to on_bars (one call per interval) when the first later bar arrives, before that bar settles, or at a quote at or after it, or at finish. Its orders wait for a later period's bar, as on_bar's do. Such a strategy is fed every bar of a period before anything later.

Parameters
eventThe bar; feed bars in timestamp order across instruments. Within one instrument and interval each bar's timestamp must be later than the last.
trading_dayWhether the bar's date trades, for the phase; empty gives UNKNOWN.
Exceptions
std::invalid_argument(with a linear algebra backend) if series_capacity_ is zero, or the bar is not later than the previous bar of its instrument and interval. The exception propagates after the bar was counted and settled; it does not set ReplayResult::halted_.
std::invalid_argument(for a strategy declaring on_bars, with or without a linear algebra backend) if the bar's period was already delivered, it is earlier than bars already fed, or its instrument and interval already have a bar for that period; thrown before anything changes.

Replays one quote: moves the price and the clock to it, re-derives the phase (calling on_phase on a change), calls on_quote, and publishes order updates.

A quote does not mark the ledger, record an equity point or fill anything. An order placed from on_quote fills against a later bar, not the quote (see ReportCaveats::quotes_). Compared with a live loop, on_phase fires before on_quote here (live, after the iteration's polls), and order updates are published here after every quote (live, only when the working-order poll runs) to a strategy that declares on_order_update. A quote before the first bar is fine; its orders wait for a bar.

Parameters
eventThe quote; feed it in timestamp order with the bars, since the clock follows whatever instant it is given, even backwards.
trading_dayWhether the quote's date trades; empty gives UNKNOWN.
Exceptions
std::invalid_argumentfor a strategy declaring on_bars, if the quote is earlier than bars already fed; a quote at or after a pending period first delivers that period to on_bars.

Ends the replay: calls on_stop (with the current portfolio, including orders from the last bar), settles what it asked for, and counts the orders still queued.

For a strategy declaring on_bars, the last period's bars are delivered first.

After a halt it does nothing; on_stop is not called.
