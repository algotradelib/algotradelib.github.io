Detailed Description

The mock broker's adaptation: what it implements, and the thunks to mock::MockClient.

It lists working orders (open orders only), reports buying power, carries the sellable quantity on its holdings rows, and answers reference data and a calendar (what the market was given, or its defaults). It refuses before calling the client: a trigger price, an idempotency key, a modification (replace_unsupported), a partial cancellation (partial_cancel_unsupported), and a history filtered by symbol or limited to a row count.
