Detailed Description

The mock broker's client: blocking, answering from a Market, after any faults scripted for the call.

Bind it like any blocking client, BlockingSession<MockClient>(std::make_shared<MockClient>(market), MockAccount{}). Nothing leaves the process; environment is "paper".

◆ repeat_last()

void algotradelib::mock::MockClient::repeat_last ( Call call,
int times = 1 )
inline

Answers the next reads of an account's state with what the last one answered, stamped now, as a broker that has not yet reported a change does.

The market moves on underneath: a fill that happened is missing from these answers, and a cancellation of an order they still list may answer nothing_to_cancel. A read with no earlier answer to repeat answers normally.

Parameters
callCall::HOLDINGS, Call::BUYING_POWER, Call::WORKING_ORDERS or Call::ORDER_HISTORY.
timesHow many reads, at least one.
Exceptions
std::invalid_argumentfor another call, or times below one.
