Detailed Description

template<typename BrokerTag>
class algotradelib::backtest::QueueingExecutor< BrokerTag >

The replay's executor: accepts orders and holds them until they fill or are withdrawn.

queued() is always the complete set of outstanding orders; the engine shows them to the strategy as working orders.

Tests whether a replay can price an order from bars.

Only regular-session limit and market orders without a trigger, and DAY stop-limit orders with both a trigger and a limit price. Best-limit, primary-limit, mid-price, conditional-limit and closing-price orders need a book or a session close that bars do not carry.
