Detailed Description

One journal line: an instruction and what was known about it when the line was written.

Every line repeats the whole instruction, so an answering line is readable on its own (with tail) and survives the loss of its PENDING line. recorded_at_ is the executor's clock (this machine's, unless one was injected), not the broker's.

◆ venue_

std::optional<Venue> algotradelib::runner::JournalEntry::venue_ {Venue::UNSPECIFIED}

The venue the order actually goes to, as the binding resolves it.

UNSPECIFIED is a recorded fact (no venue was asked for, as always at Toss). Empty means the route cannot be known from the line (an older line, an unrecognized value, or a damaged line); recovery then reports a lookalike row as ambiguous rather than matching it.
