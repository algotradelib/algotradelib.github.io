Detailed Description

template<typename BrokerTag>
struct algotradelib::runner::DryRunReport< BrokerTag >

What one runner pass decided and did, sorted by outcome (returned by Runner::feed).

The buckets list outcomes of orders, cancellations and plans, not one entry per instruction: a target already met adds nothing, and one target can produce several orders and cancellations.
