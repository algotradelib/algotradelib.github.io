◆ unreplayed_idle_limit_

std::chrono::milliseconds algotradelib::network::HttpClientOptions::unreplayed_idle_limit_ {1000}

How long a kept-alive connection may sit idle and still carry a request other than a GET (one second by default).

A server closes idle connections on its own schedule (KIS after five seconds), and a request written into a closed connection fails. A GET is then sent again, but any other request (which may be an order) is not, so its outcome would be unknown. Such a request after a longer idle period is therefore sent on a fresh connection, at the cost of a TLS handshake (once per request in AsyncHttpClient; see its description).
