Detailed Description

One commission rate, the market and venue it applies to, and the dates it applies on.

Rates are fractions of traded value (0.00015, not 0.015). An absent date bound is open-ended in that direction; a bound that was present but unreadable sets from_unreadable_ or until_unreadable_, and then applies_on answers UNREADABLE rather than treating the row as open-ended. No per-order minimum or transaction tax is modelled here; a backtest states a sale tax with backtest::NextBarFillModel::with_sell_tax.

◆ institution_rate_

std::optional<Decimal64> algotradelib::CommissionRate::institution_rate_

The institution fee (유관기관제비용), as a fraction of traded value, if stated separately.

Empty means the source gave one combined rate and said nothing about its parts (every Toss row); a report then treats rate_ as inclusive. A present zero is a stated zero; a positive value is charged in addition to rate_.
