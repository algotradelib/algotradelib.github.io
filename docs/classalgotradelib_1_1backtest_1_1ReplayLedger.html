Detailed Description

Positions, cash and realized profit, updated by each execution of a replay.

Cash and profit are kept per currency and never converted.

bool algotradelib::backtest::ReplayLedger::apply ( const Execution & execution,
const Currency & currency )
inline

Applies one execution to the position, cash and realized profit.

A trade through flat (selling 150 from a long of 100) closes the old position, realizing its profit against the old average, and opens the remainder at the new price. Commission, institution fee and tax are deducted from cash.

Parameters
executionThe execution.
currencyThe instrument's currency.
Returns
false, with the ledger unchanged, if the arithmetic does not fit, or if currency differs from the currency of a non-flat position in the instrument; true otherwise.
Note
If an allocation throws partway, the ledger can be left partly updated.

template<typename BrokerTag>
strategy::Portfolio< BrokerTag > algotradelib::backtest::ReplayLedger::snapshot ( std::chrono::system_clock::time_point as_of) const
inline

Returns the portfolio a strategy sees.

The model a replay presents: every held share is sellable (basis whole holding, deduct resting sells, unverified), buying power is this ledger's cash (no settlement is modelled), and cash_ is filled. Working orders are left empty; Backtester fills them from its queue.

Template Parameters
BrokerTagThe order-reference tag of the portfolio.
Parameters
as_ofThe snapshot time, for as_of_ and the buying-power stamps.
Returns
The portfolio.
