Detailed Description

The intraday session of a market, as the operator states it.

Times are minutes from local midnight, with a fixed UTC offset. The default is the KRX regular session, 09:00-15:30 KST.

Note
The offset is fixed: a market that observes daylight saving needs a different window each season.
