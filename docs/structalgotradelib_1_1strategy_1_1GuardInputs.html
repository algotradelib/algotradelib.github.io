std::vector<InstrumentBasis> algotradelib::strategy::GuardInputs::exposure_basis_

The currency and mark of every instrument held or working, plus the order's.

Needed only by RiskLimits::max_gross_exposure_. A missing entry, or two entries for one instrument, refuses with GuardReason::VALUE_NOT_KNOWN. The runner fills it from reference data.
