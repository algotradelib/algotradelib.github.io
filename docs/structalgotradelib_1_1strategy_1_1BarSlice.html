Detailed Description

Every instrument's bar of one interval for one period end, delivered to on_bars.

A runner calls on_bars once per period end and interval, after every bar of that period has been fed: cached (with a linear algebra backend, so ctx.bars(id, interval_) ends at period_end_ for each instrument here), priced and clocked. An instrument with no bar for the period is absent; there is no deadline and no placeholder. Several intervals ending at the same instant give one slice each.

The slice is valid only during the call.
