std::optional<TradingCalendar> algotradelib::mock::MarketSettings::calendar_

The trading days the market publishes, as a backtest is given them (backtest::ReplayConfig::calendar_).

Empty by default: then every local date from the first bar to the last, open where any instrument has a bar and closed where none has, for the country of the first instrument that has bars (KR when none has); dates outside are uncovered. The local dates are session_'s, one offset for every instrument; give the loop the same window (runner::PollingLoop::set_session_window).
