template<typename BrokerTag>
std::optional<std::chrono::system_clock::time_point> algotradelib::backtest::QueuedOrder< BrokerTag >::decided_at_

When the strategy decided the order, if known.

Where the engine knows when a bar's period began and this instant is exact, the order may fill only against a bar whose period began at or after it (so a decision at 10:30 is not filled at that day's 09:00 open). A nominal instant (decision_is_exact_ false) is an upper bound: a start at or after it still proves the order came first, while an earlier start is allowed and counted in ReplayResult::unchecked_fill_causality_. Empty (an order placed by a caller driving the executor directly) is always eligible.
