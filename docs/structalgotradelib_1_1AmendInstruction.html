Detailed Description

A modification or cancellation of a working order.

Build one from a WorkingOrder with cancel_all, cancel_quantity, replace_with or reprice, which fill in what the bound broker needs. all_remaining_ is never inferred. A broker that needs a quantity (KIS, even to cancel everything) refuses an instruction without one.
