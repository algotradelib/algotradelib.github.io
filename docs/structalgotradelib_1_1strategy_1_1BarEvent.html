Detailed Description

One bar, delivered to on_bar.

interval_ and stamp_ say which period the bar covers; period_end_ is the instant that period ended, the earliest moment the bar could be known. See bar_ for which bars a live source may deliver while still forming.

Note
Live, live::SessionSource holds back a daily or minute bar whose period has not ended, such as the broker's row for the current session. Weekly, monthly and yearly bars stamped at their open are delivered as read and may still be forming.
