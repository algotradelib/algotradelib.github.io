Detailed Description

The request rate a connection may use.

The defaults are a starting point, not a safe figure: a broker's real limit may be lower. KIS states no per-key figure; on one account, eight a second was too many for its holiday calendar, and a demo account refused reads less than about a second apart. The examples pace KIS at one a second.
