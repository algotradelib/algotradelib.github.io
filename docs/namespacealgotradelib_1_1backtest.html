◆ LimitFillPrice

enum class algotradelib::backtest::LimitFillPrice
strong

Which price a backtest credits a limit order that fills.

Enumerator
AT_LIMIT 

The default: fill at the limit, or at the bar's worst traded price when the whole bar was better than the limit.

A bar that gaps through a resting limit is not credited its open. A limit marketable through the whole bar (a buy above the high) is credited the high (a sell below the low, the low), which is better than the limit; ReportCaveats::limit_fills_bounded_ counts those fills.

AT_OPEN_IF_BETTER 

Fill at the bar's open when it opened through the limit (assumes the order was resting before the open).

Builds a flat commission model from a schedule's rate for a market on a date.

The model has no minimum (none is published) and no tax; it charges that one rate for the whole replay, even if the schedule changes rate later. For per-date rates use CommissionPolicy::scheduled.

Parameters
scheduleThe schedule, published by a broker or stated by the caller.
countryThe market.
dateThe date whose rate to use.
Returns
The model (from_broker_ true only when the schedule's origin_ is BROKER_PUBLISHED), or an INVALID_ARGUMENT error with broker code no_rate_for_date or rate_bounds_unreadable (see CommissionSchedule::RateSelection).

std::optional< math::Scalar > algotradelib::backtest::periods_per_year ( BarInterval interval,
const strategy::SessionWindow & session,
math::Scalar trading_days )
inline

Returns how many bars of an interval a year contains, for analytics::annualize_ratio.

Daily bars: trading_days. Intraday bars: the bars a session holds, times trading_days. A session that is not a whole number of bars ends with a shorter one, which counts: KRX's 390-minute session holds 390 one-minute bars (95,550 a year over 245 days) and 7 hourly ones, the last of them 30 minutes.

Parameters
intervalThe bar interval.
sessionThe session window (used for intraday bars).
trading_daysTrading days per year, such as about 245 for KRX or 252 for US markets.
Returns
The count, or std::nullopt for weekly, monthly or yearly bars, a non-positive trading_days, or (for intraday bars) a session whose close is not after its open.
