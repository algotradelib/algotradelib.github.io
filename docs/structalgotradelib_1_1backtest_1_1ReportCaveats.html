◆ bar_tape_unverified

bool algotradelib::backtest::ReportCaveats::bar_tape_unverified = true
staticconstexpr

Always true: the replay cannot know which venue's tape its bars came from.

A KRX-listed stock trades on KRX and NXT, and KIS serves either tape or the consolidated one (KisConfig::market_tape_, consolidated by default; older datasets may be KRX only). Bars carry no venue, so a replay's bars and a live run's can legitimately differ.

◆ buying_power_is_ledger_cash

bool algotradelib::backtest::ReportCaveats::buying_power_is_ledger_cash = true
staticconstexpr

Always true: a replay's buying power is the ledger's own cash, not a broker's figure.

The ledger models no settlement; a broker computes buying power on its own terms, and which way an account differs cannot be known here. With RiskLimits::enforce_buying_power_ on, the cash is reduced by what the replay's own resting buys commit (at their limit price or the last close).

◆ multiple_instruments_

bool algotradelib::backtest::ReportCaveats::multiple_instruments_ {false}

Whether more than one instrument traded: a caution about survivorship bias.

A universe built from a broker's current listings omits instruments delisted during the period, which tends to flatter the result. The engine cannot see how the universe was chosen, so this is a proxy that is also set for a hand-picked universe with no such problem. Figures are still computed.

◆ overdrawn_bars_

std::size_t algotradelib::backtest::ReportCaveats::overdrawn_bars_ {0}

Bars that closed with cash below zero in the replay's currency: the replay borrowed, which the default limits allow.

Figures measured over borrowed money are leveraged. Short sale proceeds count as cash, so leverage through shorts is not counted here. See ReplayResult::overdrawn_bars_.

◆ quotes_

std::size_t algotradelib::backtest::ReportCaveats::quotes_ {0}

How many quotes the replay was fed (zero for a bars-only replay).

Note
An order placed from on_quote does not fill against the quote: it fills against a later bar (a market order at that bar's open), at the earliest the first bar whose period began at or after the decision, where the engine can tell when a period began. Live, the same order reaches the broker at once. So a quote-driven strategy's replayed fills can be later, and at a different price, than its live ones.

◆ unchecked_fill_causality_

std::size_t algotradelib::backtest::ReportCaveats::unchecked_fill_causality_ {0}

How many times a fill was allowed without the engine being able to prove its price came after the decision.

Counted once per queued order per bar considered (the fill model may still decline). It happens when a bar's period start or end is not known exactly: a close-stamped weekly, monthly or yearly bar, or an open-stamped bar of a day or longer, whose end is nominal (every shipped adapter produces those). An open-stamped weekly series whose bars each start at or after the previous bar's nominal end (Monday to Monday) counts nothing; a close-stamped weekly series counts every decision.

With no quotes, one interval per instrument and non-overlapping periods, the fills were causal whatever this says. See ReplayResult::unchecked_fill_causality_.

Bars whose equity total could not be computed exactly (ReplayResult::unvalued_bars_).
