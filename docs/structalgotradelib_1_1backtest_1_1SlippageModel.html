Detailed Description

Slippage: how far a fill price moves against the order (a buy pays more, a sell gets less).

basis_points_ is a fraction of the price in ten-thousandths; ticks_ walks that many steps along the instrument's grid, one at a time, so a walk that crosses into another band takes that band's tick from there on. Both apply when both are set: the ticks are walked from the price before slippage, and the two amounts added.

Moves a price against the order.

Parameters
priceThe price before slippage.
sideThe order's side.
ruleThe instrument's grid. A buy steps up by the tick at each price; a sell steps down by the tick of the band below each price. From a price on the KRX grid each step reaches the next grid price; from a price off the grid, or with a custom table whose band starts are not multiples of both ticks, it may not (as with PriceRule::round_to_tick). A step the grid has no tick for (an unknown grid, a sell at the first band's start) or that would end at or below zero is not taken.
Returns
The slipped price and the amount given up, or std::nullopt if the arithmetic does not fit.
