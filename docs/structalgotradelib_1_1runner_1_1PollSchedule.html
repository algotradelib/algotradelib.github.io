Detailed Description

How often a PollingLoop polls each kind; an unset interval disables that poll.

Only calendar_ is on by default. tick_ is the loop's own step: it bounds how quickly a stop is noticed, even when no poll is due.

Note
Members are positional in aggregate initialization (PollSchedule{std::chrono::seconds(5)} sets bars_); new members are appended.

◆ calendar_

std::optional<std::chrono::nanoseconds> algotradelib::runner::PollSchedule::calendar_ {std::chrono::hours(6)}

How often to read the trading calendar; six hours by default.

Without a calendar the phase is UNKNOWN and the default RiskLimits::permitted_phases_ refuses every order that opens or increases a position (closing a position is still allowed). Keep it under the nine hours between local midnight and a 09:00 open. KIS's client caches the calendar, so four polls a day cost about one request.

◆ working_orders_

std::optional<std::chrono::nanoseconds> algotradelib::runner::PollSchedule::working_orders_

How often to read the working orders.

Do not make it shorter than holdings_: a listing newer than the holdings withholds every plan as PORTFOLIO_SUPERSEDED (equal cadences read both in one iteration, listing first). Required for a runner that sends to a broker: without it, or when the last listing is older than PollingLoop::set_maximum_portfolio_age, the loop holds placements as PORTFOLIO_STALE (leave a tick of margin between the cadence and the age).
