Detailed Description

A series split into zero-filled values and a presence mask, for sums that skip gaps.

Made by observed_parts(). Over any range, values_.segment(first, length).sum() is the sum of the observed entries and present_.segment(first, length).sum() is how many there are. Deviations from a mean work the same way, because an absent entry contributes exactly zero:

const Scalar squared = (parts.values_.segment(first, length) -
mean * parts.present_.segment(first, length)).squaredNorm();
