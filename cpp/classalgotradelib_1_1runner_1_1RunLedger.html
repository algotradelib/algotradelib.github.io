Detailed Description

One run's cash and its own shares, for strategy::RiskLimits::run_budget_.

A new run opens with the budget as its cash and no shares. Each fill moves both: a buy spends its amount and its fee and adds its quantity, a sell returns its amount less its fee and takes its quantity away. The cash may go below zero only by a fee booked above what was reserved; the shares never do.

runner::Runner keeps one when a budget is set; read it through Runner::run_cash and Runner::run_holding.
