Detailed Description

template<typename Source, typename = void>
struct algotradelib::runner::source_reports_buying_power< Source, typename >

Detects whether a source declares static constexpr bool reports_buying_power = true, meaning its broker reports buying power (BrokerCaps::has_buying_power).

false when undeclared, as for a broker that reports none: PollingLoop then holds every placement as strategy::HaltReason::BUYING_POWER_UNREAD unless strategy::RiskLimits::run_budget_ is set, and also with one while strategy::RiskLimits::enforce_buying_power_ is on (no reading will arrive), with a note naming the missing declaration. live::SessionSource declares it; a source that wraps one by composition must forward the constant.
