Detailed Description

The BrokerSession adapter for alpaca::AlpacaBlockingClient.

A US listing is InstrumentId{Country::US, Venue::UNSPECIFIED, symbol}, in dollars. Every placement is checked by alpaca::detail::validate_order (this traits' validate_place and the client's own place_order alike), then by the client's reads of the asset, the position and the clock. Replacements are refused: cancel and place instead, as a runner does. It declares no no_account(): the keys are the account, so every AlpacaAccount trades.
