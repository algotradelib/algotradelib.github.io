PriceRule algotradelib::us::equity_price_rule ( )
inline

Returns the US equity price grid: $0.01 at or above $1, $0.0001 below (Regulation NMS Rule 612).

Rule 612 sets the smallest increment an order may be priced in for an NMS stock (one listed on a US national exchange); OTC equities are not covered. The half-penny tier of the 2024 amendments, for some stocks, was not checked: if it is in effect, this grid refuses an x.xx5 limit on those stocks, which is the safe direction.

Returns
The rule.
